Design note

Read the stack from model to world, top to bottom on every screen: Pi → FireFlow → Lanni → Unfazed. Layer 02 offers FireFlow or the attached Unfazed alternative for lighter orchestration; Unfazed also retains the final deployment role at layer 04. This is a choice within one stage, not a fifth sequential layer. Bold stage labels and downward arrows explain the offering; it does not require clients to adopt every layer. Dotted connections mean optional Galactica privacy. The solid Pi connection means inherent privacy. The 50/50 label describes Pi’s joint venture, not ownership of the other products. The presets are illustrative combinations, not commercial bundles or dependency guarantees. The journey is a proposed narrative, not a customer case study or live execution. All four blocks and the separate Galactica block open their own pages.
